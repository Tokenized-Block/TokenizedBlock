Deploy BlockSkins

One transaction, signed by your wallet, creates the contract on Base. This page sends nothing by itself.

What the contract does

  • Minting a skin costs 1 USDC, sent straight to the fee wallet.
  • A skin can be sold at a fixed price or by auction (7 days at most). Each sale pays 10% to the fee wallet.
  • Sales run in ETH, USDC, or a token the owner has allowed.
  • A bid must raise the highest one by 5%. A bid in the last 5 minutes adds 5 minutes, never past the 7 days.
  • The wallet that signs here becomes the owner. The owner can only allow or disallow a payment token.
  • It cannot be changed or paused after it is deployed.
