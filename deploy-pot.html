Deploy the reward pot

One transaction, signed by your wallet. This page never sees a key. The contract has no owner and lands at the same address whoever sends it — the address depends only on its code.

What this pot is

  • It pays the people who hold a block, from a pot anyone can fill. Each round, one past block is drawn and the balances at that exact block decide the split.
  • Nobody can take the money out. There is no withdraw function in this contract — not for an owner, not for an operator, not for me. What goes in only leaves through a proven claim, or rolls over into a later round, and anyone can trigger that roll.
  • The draw cannot be picked after the fact. A round can only be opened while its end is still in the future, so the randomness that decides the drawn block does not exist yet when the round is committed. The chain enforces that one.
  • Claims wait 6 hours after a root is posted. The contract cannot check that root itself; that delay is the window where anyone can recompute it and say it is wrong.
  • What it cannot check, said plainly: that the seed really is the randomness of the end block, and that the root really matches the balances at the drawn block. Both are recomputable by anyone from public data — that is why claims are not instant.
