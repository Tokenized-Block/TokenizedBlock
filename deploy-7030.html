One transaction, signed by your wallet. This page never sees a key. The contract has no owner and lands at the same address whoever sends it: the address depends only on its code.

What this contract is

  • 0.07 % to the fee wallet + 0.03 % to the block's creator, on every swap, always in the paired currency (ETH, USDC, cbBTC, TOSHI, OUSD or a tokenized stock), never in the block.
  • 62 currencies a new block can be paired with, fixed forever at deployment: the 12 the current contract knows, plus 50 more including every tokenized stock the issuer lists today.
  • A creator keeps the 0.03 % only while a small escrow (~$1 of the paired currency) is in place; the floor of each currency is baked in.
  • Nothing changes for the app until its flag is switched on, after this address is live and re-read on chain. Markets already open keep the contract they were born with.
