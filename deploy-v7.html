Deploy the TBlock hook V7

One transaction, signed by your wallet. This page never sees a key. The contract has no owner and lands at the same address whoever sends it — the address depends only on its code.

What the V7 changes

  • The whole fee goes to the fee wallet. Until now the creator of a market kept a third of every fee on it. The V7 pays none of it.
  • What that removes, said plainly. That share was the only reason a stranger would launch a block here rather than somewhere else. It is gone.
  • What the measurement says about it. Fourteen days, 303 of 303 windows read, no window missed: two launches, both from our own wallets. Over that window the incentive brought in no outside creator at all. What it was costing is measured too — the creator share was 0.001019868 ETH over those fourteen days, so the wallet takes 50% more at the same volume.
  • What the measurement does NOT say. What would happen with outside creators. Fourteen days without them is not proof they would never come.
  • The creator is still named on chain. The market still records who opened it, and an event on every fee carries the creator who was not paid — so the change is readable in the logs, not something you have to diff two contracts to find.
  • No access control is touched. Both V4 locks are kept word for word, and the fee wallet has no special right to register a market. Two tests hold that line.
  • It repairs nothing that already exists. A hook is part of a pool's identity in Uniswap v4. Every pool already open keeps the hook it was born with.
