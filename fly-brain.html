What this model is, and is not

  • The neurons and the synapse counts between them are measured (electron microscopy). Threshold: 5 synapses; 300 Kenyon cells out of ~2,000, taken by ascending id.
  • The dynamics are ours: activity spreads in proportion to synapse counts, all excitatory except APL, modelled as global inhibition that lets only the top 10% of Kenyon cells through.
  • Which PNs receive a rise or a fall is an arbitrary choice (id parity): a fly does not smell prices.
  • Nothing here feels anything: it is a computed state, replayable by anyone with the same data.
