Claim your share

If you were holding a block when a reward round was drawn, this page finds your share and builds the claim. It never holds a key — your wallet signs.

What this page checks before it hands you anything

  • It recomputes the whole tree from the chain — the drawn block, the balances at that block, the exclusions — and compares the root it gets to the one written in the contract.
  • If the two roots disagree, you get nothing and a warning instead. A proof from a tree that does not match would fail in your wallet and cost you gas, and you would think the problem was yours.
  • It replays your own proof to the root before showing you the button.
  • Nothing here is stored on my side. Anyone can redo this with public data.
