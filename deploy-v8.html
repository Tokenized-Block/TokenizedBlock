Deploy the TBlock hook V8

One transaction, signed by your wallet. This page never sees a key. The contract has no owner and lands at the same address whoever sends it — the address depends only on its code.

What the V8 changes

  • The fee drops from 3% to 0.5%. That is the only difference from the previous hook. Everything else — the whole fee going to the fee wallet, both locks, the permissions — is carried over unchanged.
  • Why this has to be decided now, not later. The rate is a constant, and the hook is part of a pool's identity in Uniswap v4. A market opened on the previous hook pays 3% forever — there is no migration. That is exactly the trap the five oldest markets are still in.
  • What it costs, measured. Fourteen days, 303 of 303 windows read, no window missed: fees totalled 0.003059603 ETH at 3%. The same volume at 0.5% would have produced 0.000509934 ETH — six times less. Volume has to grow sixfold just to break even.
  • What nobody can measure. Whether a rate six times lower brings six times the volume. This is a bet, not a demonstrated improvement.
  • It repairs nothing that already exists. Every pool already open keeps the hook — and the rate — it was born with.
