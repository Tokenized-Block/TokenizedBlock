TokenizedBlock · Shopify skins

Skins bought on Shopify, claimed on Base.

A skin paid in USDC on the Shopify store opens a voucher. The buyer's own wallet sends the claim, and this contract mints the skin. Nothing is paid to this contract, ever. One signature from your wallet deploys it, and that wallet becomes its owner. This page sends nothing by itself.

How it works

01 · THE ORDER

Paid on Shopify

A paid order (orders/paid, checked by its HMAC) opens one voucher per skin, for the wallet the buyer wrote in the order. A test order or a refunded order opens none.

02 · THE VOUCHER

Signed by a server key

The server signs an EIP-712 voucher with a dedicated key. That key can only make vouchers: it holds no funds and never sends a transaction.

03 · THE CLAIM

The buyer's wallet mints

The claim must come from the wallet named in the voucher, before its deadline, once per order unit. The skin is then the buyer's token.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • The wallet that deploys becomes the owner. The owner can only change the signer address (a leaked key is cut at once), cancel an order that was never claimed (a refunded order), and hand ownership over in two steps.
  • The owner cannot mint, cannot move anyone's token, and holds no funds. The contract takes no USDC and no ETH; there is no fee on resale and no royalty.
  • Not upgradeable, no pause.
  • The trust point, said plainly: whoever holds the signer key can make vouchers, so free skins, for any wallet that then claims. It moves nobody's money, because this contract holds none.
