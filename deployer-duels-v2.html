TokenizedBlock · Block Duels v2

Duels where staying silent no longer pays.

Block Duels v2 is Block Duels with one rule changed. One signature from your wallet creates the v2 contract on Base, with the same parameters as v1. This page sends nothing by itself. This is a game of chance played for money: the decision to run it, and its compliance, is the owner's.

What changed from v1, and why

  • v1: a joined duel not settled within … blocks after its result block was refunded in full to both sides. The player who joins is online and sees the result first: they settled when they won, stayed silent when they lost, and got their stake back. Over 2,000 simulated duels that strategy gave the joiner +454.4 USDC and cost the creator 568 USDC (forge test test_V_DuelEV).
  • v2: a joined duel not settled within that window is won by default by the creator. Anyone can call it (settleDefault) once the window has passed; it pays exactly like a creator win: …% to the creator, …% to the community reward pool, …% to TokenizedBlock (plus rounding dust). On the same 2,000 duels, staying silent when losing gives the joiner exactly what settling every duel gives.
  • A tie settled inside the window still refunds both stakes with no fee. A tie not settled in the window goes to the creator by default. So the player who joins must settle a win or a tie in time.
  • The window is … blocks: about 8.5 minutes at 2-second blocks, about 51 seconds at 200 ms blocks (Denim). The app measures the current block time and says it before a join is signed.
  • A duel nobody joined is still refunded to its creator once it expires; the creator can still cancel an open duel. Nothing else changed: create, join, settle inside the window, the face, the split, the admitted tokens.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No owner, no withdrawal, no pause, no upgrade, no fee change. Stakes leave only through a settled duel, a duel won by default, a cancelled open duel (its creator), or the refund of an expired open duel.
  • The guardian can only admit or remove a token for new duels. It cannot touch any stake. USDC is admitted for good.
  • If a token refuses a payment to one address, the others are still paid; the refused amount stays owed to that address, which can claim it to another address.
  • The face comes from the hash of a Base block, computed on chain exactly like v1 and Block Rolls. A missing block hash is never used to decide a duel. The Base sequencer, which builds the blocks, is the one party that could choose a hash.
