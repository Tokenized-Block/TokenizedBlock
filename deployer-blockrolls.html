TokenizedBlock · Block Rolls

Guess the face of the next block.

A roll is a guess on the five traits of the face that a Base block, two blocks ahead, will reveal. One signature from your wallet opens the game on Base. This page sends nothing by itself. This is a paid game of chance: the decision to run it, and its compliance, is the owner's.

How it works

01 · THE ROLL

… USDC a roll

…% goes to the prize pot, …% to TokenizedBlock at once. You pick five traits; the block two ahead decides.

02 · THE WIN

Prizes from the roll pot

… USDC for 5 traits in common, … for 4, … for 3, … for 2 — never more than the pot holds. Fewer than … traits pays nothing.

03 · THE PASS

… USDC a pass

… rolls for … hours, and a ticket in that day's draw: … USDC goes to the day pot, … to TokenizedBlock. One ticket per wallet per day; the whole day pot goes to one buyer.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No owner and no withdraw function: the roll pot leaves only through winning rolls, the day pot only through the day's draw.
  • No refund. A roll not settled within … blocks after its target block is lost — about 8.5 minutes today, about 51 seconds once Base moves to 200 ms blocks (Denim). Anyone can settle any roll, in batches.
  • The prizes and the minimum winning score can be changed, but only by the creator of UNIT as the TokenizedBlock hook names it, within hard bounds (at most 1000 USDC a prize, a higher tier never pays less than a lower one, minimum score 2 to 5), and only for rolls made after the change. The creator has no withdraw call, but nothing stops that wallet from raising the prizes and then rolling itself, so the roll pot is not out of its reach.
  • The face comes from the hash of a Base block. No player, no server and no creator chooses it; the Base sequencer, which builds the blocks, is the one party that could. The same trust as today's draw.
  • The face is computed on chain exactly like the app computes it: the same keccak, the same six colour bands, ten orbits, nineteen facets, nine materials and eight ornaments.
