Privacy policy

TokenizedBlock · tokenizedblock.space · last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

TokenizedBlock is a self-custodial app on the Base blockchain. We do not create accounts, we do not ask for your name, email or phone, and we never hold your funds or your keys. Every transaction is signed in your own wallet.

What we receive

  • Your public wallet address, when you connect a wallet, so the app can read your balances and prepare transactions for you to sign. Wallet addresses and transactions on Base are public by nature.
  • Technical request data (IP address, browser user agent, time of request) that our hosting provider processes to serve the site and keep it running.
  • Anonymous usage counters (for example how many times a "Buy" button was clicked), used to see what works. They are not tied to your name.
  • Messages and settings you publish on chain (for example a block's voice or a message in a transfer) are public and permanent by design.

What stays in your browser

Preferences such as the block you last opened, your theme or panel sizes are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you send them, and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Your wallet provider (for example Coinbase Wallet / Base Account or another wallet you choose) handles signing under its own policy.
  • Base network nodes (RPC providers) receive the read requests the app makes about public chain data.
  • Card and bank on-ramps — Coinbase Onramp and MoonPay. If you choose to buy crypto with a card or a bank transfer, you leave our app for theirs: they collect what they need for payments and identity checks (KYC) under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card or identity details.
  • Market data such as DexScreener may be loaded to show prices and charts.
  • Hosting: the site is served by Railway.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not build profiles of you.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without connecting a wallet. Data you published on a public blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone, including us. For any other request (access, correction, deletion of data we hold), contact us below and we will answer within 30 days.
