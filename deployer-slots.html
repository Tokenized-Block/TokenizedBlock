TokenizedBlock · Block Slots

Three block faces. One spin.

A spin draws three block faces from three different Base blocks — two, three and four blocks ahead, one face each. Faces that match, trait by trait, score points; the contract judges the spin when it is settled and pays a fixed prize from the slots pot itself. One signature from your wallet opens the game on Base. This page sends nothing by itself. This is a paid game of chance: the decision to run it, and its compliance, is the owner's.

How it works

01 · THE SPIN

… USDC a spin

…% goes to the slots pot and …% (… USDC) to TokenizedBlock, at the spin, whatever happens next. A spin not settled in time is lost: nothing is returned. The three faces come from three different blocks, each face from its own block hash.

02 · THE POINTS

Trait by trait

For each of the five traits: all three faces the same scores 3, two the same scores 1. Up to 15 points a spin. Your points add up on chain as a running total; they pay nothing on their own — prizes are paid per spin.

03 · THE PRIZE

Up to … USDC a spin, capped by the pot

A fixed prize per points of the spin, from 0 to … USDC, never more than the pot holds. …% of spins pay something, but only …% pay back at least the … USDC stake. On average a settled spin pays back … USDC, less while the pot holds less than … USDC. A spin left unsettled pays nothing, so settling every spin is the best play: … USDC a spin on average, below the price.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No owner. Nobody can take the pot: it pays out only through settled spins, to the player of the spin. The prize table cannot be changed after deployment.
  • The contract refuses to deploy a table whose average payout is not strictly below the share that enters the pot: on average a spin grows the pot.
  • At most 5 USDC a spin: the contract refuses a prize table above that. A prize is paid in full or not at all: when the pot cannot cover a spin's prize, settling it is refused and a batch leaves it out, for everyone, the player included. Only the spin's player can accept what the pot holds, for that one spin, and the contract refuses if the pot has dropped below the amount they saw. Nobody can close a win at a discount without the player's own signature.
  • No refund. A spin not settled in time is lost: nothing is returned, and nothing stays stuck — at the spin its price is already split between the pot and the TokenizedBlock share. A spin is settled once its three blocks exist, and not later than … blocks after its first block — about … minutes at 2-second blocks, about … seconds once Base moves to 200 ms blocks (Denim). Why no refund: the block hashes are public, so a player knows a spin's result before choosing to settle it or let it pass; any refund of a missed window would pay the player for passing on losing spins (a full refund would return more than the price on average). Anyone can settle any spin, in batches, and the prize always goes to the spin's player: the server settles spins automatically when its keeper is on (a wallet the owner funds for gas; it can only send settles to this contract).
  • The TokenizedBlock share of each spin is sent to the fixed TokenizedBlock wallet by a call anyone can make; it never takes from the pot.
  • The faces come from the hashes of three Base blocks, one face each. No player, no server and no owner chooses them; the Base sequencer, which builds the blocks, is the one party that could.
  • Each face is computed on chain exactly like the app draws a block face: the same keccak, six colour bands, ten orbits, nineteen facets, nine materials and eight ornaments.
