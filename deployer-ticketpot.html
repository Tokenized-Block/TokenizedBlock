TokenizedBlock · Ticket Pot

The day's fees, shared by the day's ticket holders.

One pot of USDC per UTC day. The day's community share of the fees goes in; once the day is closed, every wallet that held that day's ticket can take an equal share. One signature from your wallet opens it on Base. This page sends nothing by itself.

How it works

01 · FUND

A pot per day

Anyone adds USDC to today's pot or to a past day that is not closed yet. Nothing ever comes back out to the funder.

02 · CLOSE

The pot freezes

The owner closes a finished day; anyone can after … hours. After that the pot cannot grow, so every share is final.

03 · CLAIM

Equal shares

Each ticket holder gets pot ÷ tickets, once, within … days of the day's end. What is left then rolls into the current day's pot.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No withdrawal, for anyone. USDC leaves the contract only through a claim carrying the verifier's signature, and goes to the ticket holder named in it, never to whoever sends it.
  • One claim per wallet per day; the claims of a day can never add up to more than its frozen pot.
  • The verifier is the TokenizedBlock server key. It signs each share after the day closes; it never holds or moves funds. A leaked key could sign shares only out of closed day pots: the guardian can pause claims at once.
  • Replacing the verifier or the guardian is announced on chain and takes effect only … hours later. The guardian pauses claims; it cannot lift the owner's pause, and the owner waits … hours to lift the guardian's.
  • The wallet that deploys becomes the owner: it can close days early, pause, and propose changes. It cannot take the pots.
  • The ticket is the 20-byte id the app derives from your wallet and the day; it is written into each claim event as the proof of who held that day's ticket.
