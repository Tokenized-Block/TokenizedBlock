TokenizedBlock · Missions

Your block is your pass.

Holding a block's units proves you're part of its crew and you play on TokenizedBlock. Missions turn that into rewards. One transaction, signed by your wallet, opens the mission board on Base — this page sends nothing by itself.

How a mission works

01 · THE PASS

Hold the block

Only wallets that hold the block's units can take its missions. The pass is the block itself, nothing else.

02 · THE MISSION

The creator posts a quest

The block's creator sets the reward aside up front — in the block's units, or in the stock or token the block is paired with (AMZN for a block paired with AMZN).

03 · THE WIN

Do it, keep holding

Do the mission and keep the block — and the paired asset when the mission asks — through the whole window (1 to 7 days). Selling early ends the run.

Where each reward goes

Player 90%
Player who did it: 90% Back to the creator: 3% Community reserve: 5.6% Dev: 1.4%, in USDC only

No converter set: the dev's 1.4% of a reward paid in units or a stock goes to the community reserve too — the dev never holds block units.

Why it is safe to play

  • A reward is locked the moment the mission is posted. It only leaves to players the mission checker approved, or back to the creator for what was not won. The owner cannot touch it.
  • The mission checker is a key held by TokenizedBlock: it only approves a wallet after reading on chain that the mission was done and the holding lasted the whole window. Each mission and each day has a cap.
  • Changing the key that checks missions, or the guardian, is announced 48 hours ahead while everything stays open, so every creator can take their reward back first.
  • A guardian can pause new missions; taking rewards back never pauses.
  • The wallet that signs here becomes the owner. It cannot touch any reward put aside.
