TokenizedBlock · TAM

Mathematical works, stored on chain.

One token per work. Minting costs 5 USDC, paid straight to the TokenizedBlock fee wallet. The work's points are written on Base, readable by anyone. One signature from your wallet deploys the contract. This page sends nothing by itself.

How it works

01 · PAY

5 USDC, once

The minter approves 5 USDC; the mint moves them from the minter to the fee wallet in the same call. The contract never holds any.

02 · STORE

The bytes, not a hash

The encoded points are written as the code of a small contract. A hash cannot be decoded; the stored bytes are what makes the work readable.

03 · REPLAY

The proof is public

Anyone can rerun the public engine with the stored params and compare it with the stored bytes. The contract itself cannot tell whether a work is chaotic or brain-made.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No owner. No admin, pause, upgrade or withdrawal. Deploying gives your wallet no power over the contract.
  • The price (5 USDC), the USDC address and the fee wallet are written in at deployment and can never change.
  • The same bytes cannot be minted twice.
  • The contract checks only the format header and the sizes. What a work is (chaotic, certified, chosen by the brain) is shown by the public replay in the app, not by the contract.
