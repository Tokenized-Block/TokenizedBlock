TokenizedBlock · Block Duels

Two wallets, one block, five traits.

A duel is two guesses on the five traits of the face a Base block will reveal. Each side puts the same stake, in USDC or in an admitted tokenized stock. One signature from your wallet opens the duels on Base. This page sends nothing by itself. This is a game of chance played for money: the decision to run it, and its compliance, is the owner's.

How it works

01 · THE CHALLENGE

Stake and guess

The creator stakes at least … raw units of an admitted token and picks five traits. The duel stays open from … minutes to … days.

02 · THE ANSWER

Same stake, own guess

Another wallet joins with the same stake and its own five traits. The face of the block … blocks later decides. A block's brain can propose a duel to its owner; the owner's wallet signs.

03 · THE SPLIT

More traits in common wins

…% of both stakes to the winner, …% to the community reward pool, …% to TokenizedBlock (plus rounding dust). A tie returns both stakes in full, with no fee.

What is fixed, and what is not

  • No owner, no withdrawal, no upgrade, no fee change. Stakes leave only through a settled duel, a cancelled open duel (its creator), or a refund.
  • No funds can stay stuck. A joined duel not settled within … blocks after its target block is refunded in full to both sides, by anyone: about 8.5 minutes today, about 51 seconds once Base moves to 200 ms blocks (Denim). So the winner, the app or anyone must settle in time, or the duel becomes a refund. A duel nobody joined is refunded to its creator once it expires.
  • The guardian can only admit or remove a token for new duels. It cannot touch any stake, and removing a token never blocks settling, cancelling or refunding a duel that already uses it. USDC is admitted for good.
  • If a token refuses a payment to one address, the others are still paid; the refused amount stays owed to that address, which can claim it to another address.
  • The face comes from the hash of a Base block, computed on chain exactly like Block Rolls and the app compute it. No player, no server and no guardian chooses it; the Base sequencer, which builds the blocks, is the one party that could.
