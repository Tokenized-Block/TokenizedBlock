      Brain AI

      128 spiking neurons per block, seeded by its address. Not an LLM — feeds on market + chain, moves the block, signs nothing. Pick any listed block — TBLOCK is not forced.

      — 0 ticks

      Each column is one beat, each dot a neuron that fired.

      ≋ — Hz left ≋ — Hz right ⤳ — steering ⚡ — spikes since open ◉ — firing now 🧠 — memory

      What these numbers mean
      • Hz left / right — how fast each wing beats. Each wing listens to a different third of the neurons.
      • Steering — left minus right, from −1 to 1. Negative turns left, positive turns right. It is what moves the block on the map.
      • Spikes since open — every neuron firing counted since you opened this block here.
      • Firing now — neurons that fired on this beat, out of 128. More than 8 makes it curious.
      • Memory — each neuron keeps a trace of what it just did, and that trace primes it on the next beats. It fades in about thirty beats, it is kept in this browser, and the brain resumes where it was when you come back.
      • Its food — its market, plus what the chain shows in the recent window: transfers, holders reached and messages. The creation itself does not count.
      • Death — only when its creator held it and now holds none. A creator who never held it, or a balance we could not read, never kills a block.

                TokenizedBank 🟦
                
      
                A Tokenized Block is a fund wallet. Graft blocks onto TokenizedBank to hold them —
        then open USDC credit from on-chain proof of hold on the whole linked basket, not traditional underwriting.
        You must keep holding to maintain credit. Losing one block does not end it — surviving linked blocks still back it.
        Credit contracts with Σ hold (dies only if zero survivors).
        Early holders (snapshot at Open) share yield — badge separate from grafting.
        After Open, any wallet with proof may graft again to add liquidity to the same basket.
        The agent runs the ops — you only one-click confirm.
