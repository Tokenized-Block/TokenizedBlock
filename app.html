Brain AI

128 spiking neurons per block, seeded by its address. It watches its own market, moves its block, and signs nothing.

— 0 ticks

Each column is one beat, each dot a neuron that fired.

≋ — Hz left ≋ — Hz right ⤳ — steering ⚡ — spikes since open ◉ — firing now
  • Hz left / right — how fast each wing beats. Each wing listens to a different third of the neurons.
  • Steering — left minus right, from −1 to 1. Negative turns left, positive turns right. It is what moves the block on the map.
  • Spikes since open — every neuron firing counted since you opened this block here.
  • Firing now — neurons that fired on this beat, out of 128. More than 8 makes it curious.
  • Its food — its market, plus what the chain shows in the recent window: transfers, holders reached and messages. The creation itself does not count.
  • Death — only when its creator held it and now holds none. A creator who never held it, or a balance we could not read, never kills a block.

What “life” actually is

Life =price × supply, both re-readable on chain. Nobody paints it.
It goes downIf the price falls, life falls with it. A big block can become a small one overnight. Nobody refunds that.
⚠️ It is buyableMeasured 2026-09-09: 0.01 ETH moved a displayed market cap by 2 346 ETH — a lever of ×234 645, because supply is enormous. A big number here is not a big pile of money.
UnitMeasured in ETH, not dollars. We name the currency instead of mixing two.
No market yetA faded, smaller block has never been traded. That is not the same as being worthless.
